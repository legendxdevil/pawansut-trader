### What are Cookies and Local Storage?
Cookies and browser local storage are small text snippets and data keys saved on your device by websites to provide necessary functionality, retain cart items, and remember your session preferences.

### What We Use
Pawansut Trader utilizes **strictly essential storage only**:
- **Shopping Cart (`pt_cart_v1`)**: Stores your selected product IDs, variant choices, and item quantities so your basket persists across pages and browser sessions.
- **Wishlist (`pt_wishlist_v1`)**: Saves your bookmarked products.
- **Cookie Consent (`pt_cookie_ok`)**: Remembers that you acknowledged our essential storage notification.
- **Admin Session Cookie**: A secure, encrypted `httpOnly`, `sameSite=lax` session cookie used exclusively for administrative authentication.

We do **NOT** use tracking pixels, cross-site profiling cookies, or third-party marketing trackers.

### Third-Party Payment Services
When you choose to make an online payment, our payment gateway partner (Cashfree Payments) may set necessary operational cookies on its hosted checkout iframe/page for fraud prevention and session integrity. Please refer to Cashfree's privacy policy for details.

### How to Control Local Storage
You can clear or disable cookies and local storage through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling browser storage will prevent the shopping cart and wishlist from functioning properly.

### Policy Updates
If we introduce non-essential analytics or advertising cookies in future versions, we will update this document and present an explicit opt-in consent banner.
